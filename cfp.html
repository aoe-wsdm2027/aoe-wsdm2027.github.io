Call for Papers

Submissions on agents, e-commerce AI systems, interactive environments, datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation.

About the workshop

AOE studies e-commerce AI as a coupled system: agents support consumers and businesses, agents improve models and pipelines, and agent actions reshape the environments and evidence used for future optimization.

We invite empirical, theoretical, systems, benchmark, dataset, and position work that advances shared abstractions, evaluation protocols, infrastructure, and design principles for systems that improve through agents while preserving human direction, empirical validity, and operational accountability.

Topics of interest

Interaction Loop — agents working with e-commerce participants

  • Shopping, product-search, recommendation, comparison, negotiation, and decision-making agents.
  • Merchant, advertiser, campaign-management, creator, customer-service, and operations agents.
  • Goal and preference elicitation under ambiguity, sparsity, conflict, and drift.
  • Personalized memory, profile construction, provenance, editing, deletion, and privacy.
  • Tool use, multi-step planning, mixed-initiative interaction, and human delegation.
  • Multi-agent coordination, trust, explanations, consent, and human override.

System Optimization Loop — agents improving e-commerce AI systems

  • Auto-research: problem discovery, hypothesis generation, literature and code understanding, and experiment design.
  • Search, recommendation, and advertising harnesses connecting agents to data, code, training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring tools.
  • Automated feature engineering, architecture search, bidding, budget pacing, campaign and policy optimization, debugging, and root-cause analysis.
  • Persistent research memory, experience replay, skill libraries, and transfer across tasks or domains.
  • Self-reflection, self-correction, self-evolution, and continual improvement under resource constraints.
  • Evaluation, monitoring, human oversight, and safe exploration.

Agent–Environment Loop — stateful environments for learning and evaluation

  • Sandboxed environments with executable search, recommendation, advertising, auction, transaction, and customer-service tools.
  • User, merchant, creator, and marketplace simulation, including language world models and digital twins.
  • Stateful catalogs, inventory, pricing, promotion, ad auctions, bids, budgets, exposure, orders, fulfillment, returns, and platform policies.
  • Simulator calibration, behavioral fidelity, controllability, and sim-to-real validation.
  • Counterfactual interventions, rare-event generation, stress testing, and safe exploration.
  • Long-horizon behavior, multi-stakeholder value, robustness, fairness, privacy, manipulation, and reward hacking.

Submission categories

4–8 pages

Research / System Papers

New methods, deployed systems, empirical studies, datasets, benchmarks, evaluation protocols, and well-grounded negative results.

4–8 pages

Position / Demo Papers

Forward-looking perspectives, research agendas, work-in-progress systems, and demonstration contributions.

Page limits exclude references. Submissions should use the ACM sigconf format. Reviewing will be single-blind to support discussion of deployed industrial systems while maintaining a rigorous conflict-of-interest process.

★
Best Paper Award

An outstanding accepted contribution will receive the AOE@WSDM 2027 Best Paper Award. Selection details will be announced with the final program.

In-person participation: at least one author of each accepted paper must register, attend, and present in Hong Kong. Remote presentations will not be supported.

Review Process

Each submission will receive at least two reviews assessing relevance, technical quality, clarity, and potential value to the workshop discussion. Organizer submissions will be handled by independent reviewers and non-conflicted chairs.
